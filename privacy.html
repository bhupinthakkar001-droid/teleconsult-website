Privacy Notice

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · Version: telemedicine-privacy-v1-2026-10-30

This notice explains how information is collected and used when you request an online consultation with Dr. Bhupin Thakkar through this website.

1. Information collected

When you submit a booking request, the website may collect:

  • Your name and mobile number.
  • Your selected appointment date, time and consultation type, plus a booking reference and booking status.
  • Your presenting complaint and how long you have had it.
  • Any existing diseases or past medical history that you choose to provide. This field is optional.
  • Your consent choices and the date/time and version of the consent notice.

2. Why the information is used

Your information is used to receive and manage your appointment request, contact you about the booking, prepare for and provide the consultation, arrange follow-up where appropriate, and maintain clinical and administrative records. It is not collected through this form for unrelated advertising.

3. Where information is stored and who may access it

Booking and intake information is processed through Google Apps Script and stored in a Google Sheet linked to the booking system. The doctor accesses booking details through a password-protected dashboard. Google provides the underlying hosting, application and storage services and may process information as necessary to provide those services under its applicable terms and privacy practices.

If you choose to open and send the pre-filled WhatsApp message, your name, mobile number, appointment details and booking reference are shared with the doctor through WhatsApp. The website's pre-filled booking message does not include the complaint or optional medical-history text. WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices apply to information handled through that service.

For a confirmed video consultation, a Google Meet link may be created and shared with you. Google Meet is subject to Google's terms and privacy practices.

Your information is not sold. It is shared only as needed to provide and administer the requested service, operate the technology used for it, or comply with a legal obligation.

4. Security and confidentiality

Access to the doctor dashboard is password-protected. Reasonable access and security measures should be maintained for the linked Google account and spreadsheet. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure; please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary messaging channels.

5. How long information is kept

Booking information and clinical records are retained for as long as reasonably required to manage the booking, provide continuity of care, maintain medical records and meet applicable professional or legal record-keeping requirements. Information that is no longer required will be deleted or securely disposed of, subject to any applicable retention obligation.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You may ask to access or correct booking information, raise a privacy concern, or withdraw consent for future use where applicable. Withdrawal does not undo processing already carried out lawfully, and some clinical records may need to be retained for professional or legal record-keeping. To make a privacy request, contact the doctor using the WhatsApp contact shown on the booking confirmation and include your booking reference. Please do not include unnecessary medical details in the first message.

7. Teleconsultation limitations and emergencies

Online consultation may limit physical examination and the information available to the doctor. The doctor may recommend an in-person assessment, investigations or another mode of consultation when clinically necessary. This booking service is not an emergency service. In a medical emergency, seek immediate in-person emergency care or call 112 in India.

8. Consultation recording

This booking form does not record your consultation. If recording is proposed, separate prior consent will be requested before recording begins.

9. Children and patients unable to give consent

Where the patient is a minor or is unable to provide valid consent, a parent or legally appropriate caregiver must be involved. The doctor may verify the caregiver's identity and authority before proceeding with the consultation.

Questions or concerns?

Contact Dr. Bhupin Thakkar using the WhatsApp contact provided with your booking confirmation. Quote your booking reference so your request can be located.

This notice may be updated when the service or applicable legal requirements change. Please review the latest version when making a new booking.
